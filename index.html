About Me

I'm a Java Backend Developer with 4 years of experience building and enhancing production applications using Java, Spring Boot, Spring MVC, REST APIs, Hibernate/JPA, and PostgreSQL.

At General Motors (via Tata Consultancy Services), I contributed to designing and developing an automotive diagnostics backend application from scratch — taking ownership of individual features from requirement analysis through implementation, testing, and production release. I personally optimized PostgreSQL queries and Hibernate/JPA data-access logic, reducing database query execution time from approximately 4 minutes to 0.9 seconds, and cut API response time from approximately 5 minutes to 1 minute.

I also integrated OAuth2-based Single Sign-On using Microsoft accounts, maintained Jenkins CI/CD automation and Python-based scripts for build, test, and reporting workflows, and worked on Jira automation and administration alongside cross-functional Agile/Scrum teams.

Beyond my day-to-day work, I have hands-on experience with Apache Kafka and Redis through personal backend projects. I hold a B.Tech in Electronics & Communication Engineering from Koneru Lakshmaiah University (CGPA: 8.6/10, 2022) and industry certifications from Oracle (Java Foundations) and Confluent (Apache Kafka).
