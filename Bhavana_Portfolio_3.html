About Me

I'm a Backend Engineer with 3.8 years of professional experience designing and delivering scalable, high-performance distributed systems. I specialise in Java, Spring Boot, Apache Kafka and microservices architecture.

At Tata Consultancy Services (TCS) working for a global automotive OEM, I built the ODX Automotive Diagnostic Platform — processing millions of vehicle diagnostic records daily with 99.9% uptime. I reduced database query time by 78% and overall API response time by 80% through deep PostgreSQL query optimisation, multi-level indexing, and Hibernate/JPA tuning.

I hold a B.Tech in Electronics & Communication Engineering from Koneru Lakshmaiah University (CGPA: 8.6/10, 2022) and industry certifications from Oracle (Java Foundations) and Confluent (Apache Kafka).
